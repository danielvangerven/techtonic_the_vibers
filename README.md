# KBC Ahead: user manual

KBC Ahead looks at what's coming up in your life, such as a trip abroad, a move or a wedding, and helps you prepare for it before it happens. It reads your calendar and your recent payments, spots the big moments in the months ahead, and shows you:

- a short checklist of what you already have covered and what's still missing,
- what people in a similar situation typically spent,
- one clear suggestion to close the most important gap.

All customers and numbers in this version are made up for demonstration purposes.

Once signed in, the top bar shows who you are. Use **Switch to …** to jump to the other demo customer, or **Log out** to go back to the sign-in page.

## The three pages

The top bar has three tabs: **Upcoming**, **Calendar** and **Transactions**.

### Upcoming

This is the home page. It lists the moments KBC Ahead found, split into **Next 90 days** and **Later**. The first upcoming moment opens automatically.

Each moment is shown as a card with:

- **The type and timing**, for example *Trip abroad · in 3 weeks*.
- **The title and dates**, for example *Tokyo, Japan* and the travel dates.
- **A readiness badge**: *2/4 ready* means two of the four things on the checklist are covered. It changes to **Ready** once everything is in order.

Click a card to open it. Inside you'll find:

- **The checklist.** A green tick means you're covered, an orange warning means something is missing that others often forget, and an empty circle is a to-do.
- **What people like you spent**: the typical amount similar customers spent on a comparable moment.
- **A suggestion**, such as travel insurance or a gift pool, with an **Add** button (showing the price where there is one). Clicking **Add** activates it, and the checklist updates straight away.
- **Why?** shows the reasoning: how many people like you forgot this item, why this suggestion fits, the range most people spent, and where the moment was found (calendar, payments or something you told us).
- **Not relevant** removes the moment if it's wrong or you don't need help with it.

#### Telling KBC about something yourself

Not everything is in your calendar. At the top of the Upcoming page, type what's coming up in the **Anything else coming up?** box, for example *"We're renovating the kitchen in spring"*, and click **Tell KBC**. If it recognises a moment, it's added to your list.

### Calendar

A monthly calendar with all your events.

- Use **Today** and the arrows to move between months.
- Click **New event** or an empty spot on any day to add an event. Give it a title, pick the dates, and either choose times or tick **All day**.
- Click an existing event to change or delete it.

Event colours:

| Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Solid blue | Part of an upcoming moment |
| Light blue | Any other event |
| Grey with a lock | Private: KBC Ahead never looks at it |

When you add or change an event that looks like a trip, a move or a wedding (for example *"Flight to Barcelona"*), a message tells you a new moment was found and it appears on the Upcoming page. Changing the dates of such an event also updates the moment.

### Transactions

A list of your recent payments with date, description and amount. Payments that point to something coming up, such as a flight booking or a deposit, show which moment they belong to in the **Linked moment** column.

## Your privacy

- Events that look personal or sensitive (such as medical appointments or religious services) are marked private with a lock and are never analysed.
- Comparisons with "people like you" are only shown when they are based on a group of at least 50 customers, so no individual can be singled out.
- You stay in control: remove any moment with **Not relevant**, and edit or delete any calendar event.

## Frequently asked questions

**I don't see any moments.**
Add a trip, a move or a wedding to your calendar, or tell KBC about it using the box on the Upcoming page.

**A moment is wrong.**
Open the card and click **Not relevant**. If it came from a calendar event, you can also edit or delete that event.

**My changes are gone.**
Signing in (including switching customers) resets the demo to its starting point.

**Why is there no "spent by people like you" amount on a card?**
There weren't enough similar customers to make a fair comparison, so none is shown.
